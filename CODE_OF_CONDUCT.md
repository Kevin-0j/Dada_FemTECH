# Code of conduct

Dada exists to help women who are often not listened to. We expect the same respect in how we
work together.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or to writing code.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Respect people's lived experience. Community health promoters and mothers often know things the
  code and the research do not.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep private anything that could identify a user, a patient or a survivor.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexist, racist, homophobic, ableist or otherwise discriminatory language.
- Making light of mental illness, suicide or violence.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact details on the repository
profile. We will treat every report in confidence, respond within 5 working days and may remove
content or block people who break these rules.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
